CONSULTING SERVICE (new, hourly-billed)

Availability: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday only, two time bands per day:
- 8:00–9:00 AM WAT (West Africa Time)
- 7:00–11:00 PM WAT (for clients in other time zones)

Booking flow:
1. Client selects an open slot within the available days/bands (calendar shows only real 
   availability, blocks taken slots)
2. Client fills a form: what they want consulted on, how they want to be involved, 
   number of hours needed, preferred meeting mode (WhatsApp, email, Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams)
3. Client pays for the requested hours upfront
4. Booking confirms only after payment clears

Pricing: benchmarked to industry standard. Reference points — Brand Envoy's existing project 
rate sits at $50–150/hr (Clutch, TechBehemoths, DesignRush); general market rate for experienced 
marketing/branding strategic consulting runs $100–300/hr. Consider a two-tier rate: standard for 
Nigeria/Africa clients, higher for international clients. Final rate TBD by Temple.

Technical build note: needs a real calendar/booking system (accurate availability, slot-blocking, 
payment-gated confirmation) plus a payment gateway (Paystack). Both are necessary exceptions to 
the site's "no third-party dependency" rule — flagged intentionally.

WHO WE SERVE (full page copy)

WHO WE SERVE

Brand Envoy Isn't For Every Brand

We help brands get seen, get trusted, and get bought — across Nigeria and beyond. That takes a 
specific kind of client, ready to build something real with us. If that's you, keep reading. If 
it's not, we'll save us both the call.

Expects us to finance their market entry
You're not a fit if you're looking for a distributor to pay to land your product before you've 
committed anything yourself.
→ Already in-market, or bringing the product in themselves
You're a fit if you have a local presence already, or you're ready to bring your own product in — 
we take it from there into stores and build the campaign around it.

Treats the agency relationship as transactional
You're not a fit if you see us as a vendor to squeeze on price and drop the moment something gets hard.
→ Sees this as a partnership
You're a fit if you want a long-term relationship — some of our engagements have run for years 
because the work kept compounding.

Wants the same playbook everyone else is running
You're not a fit if every idea needs three case studies from other Nigerian agencies before you'll 
try it. Some of our best results came from doing what nobody else in this market was doing yet.
→ Ready to move first
You're a fit if you're willing to back an idea because it's right for your brand, not because it's 
already been done by someone else.

Believes the product sells itself
You're not a fit if leadership treats marketing as an afterthought — a small budget thrown at "the 
marketing stuff" while expecting the product to carry the business alone.
→ Knows the brand is part of the business, not decoration on top of it
You're a fit if leadership sees getting seen and trusted as core to growth, not a side expense.

Wants to dabble or test small
You're not a fit if you want a one-off post or a single campaign to see what happens.
→ Ready to build something that compounds
You're a fit if you're thinking in campaigns and market presence, not single posts.

Treats brand-building as a checkbox
You're not a fit if getting on billboards, into stores, or in front of press is just something to 
tick off before moving on.
→ Sees visibility as the goal, not the formality
You're a fit if you want to actually be known, remembered, and chosen in your market — not just 
technically "have marketing."

In crisis, looking for a rescue
You're not a fit if you're bleeding money and need a turnaround, not a campaign. We'd be doing you 
a disservice taking your money right now.
→ Growing, and looking for the next leg up
You're a fit if your numbers are stable or climbing and you're ready to push further, not stop the 
bleeding.

No product-market fit yet
You're not a fit if customers aren't sticking and the product itself is still finding its footing. 
We can't fix that with a campaign.
→ Has something worth telling the world about
You're a fit if you've got a real product or brand and now need more of the right people to see it.

Shopping for the cheapest quote
You're not a fit if you're comparing five agencies purely on price.
→ Knows what success is worth
You're a fit if you know what winning this market means for your business and you're ready to 
invest at the level that requires.

Running for office without a real plan (political campaigns specifically)
You're not a fit if you're exploring a candidacy with no timeline, no budget, and no urgency.
→ A serious aspirant for 2027 and beyond
You're a fit if you're building a real campaign — local government to presidential — and want a 
team that's done this since 2015.
